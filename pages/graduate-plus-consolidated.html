Track Overview & Challenge

Road Damage Detection under Various Weather Conditions

Track Overview & Challenge

The Graduate+ Track is designed for advanced students, researchers, and professionals ready to push the boundaries of AI in real-world scenarios. This year’s challenge: robust road damage detection under diverse weather conditions. Participants will develop state-of-the-art models that can accurately identify and localize road damage in images from multiple countries and weather environments, simulating the complexities of real-world deployment.


Research Focus

Emphasis on experimentation, and potential for academic publication.


Advanced Methods

Utilize state-of-the-art AI/ML techniques, including GANs, domain adaptation, and advanced augmentation.

Challenge Summary

Your mission: Build a robust AI model that detects and localizes road damage in images from a variety of countries and weather conditions (e.g., snow, rain, sunny, night). Your model should generalize well to unseen environments and lighting. The challenge uses the full Road Dataset with a focus on weather diversity.

  • Input: Road scene images from multiple countries and weather conditions
  • Output: For each damage, predict its type and bounding box (class x y w h)
  • Focus: Robustness to weather, domain adaptation, and generalization

About the Dataset

The Road Dataset for this track includes images from all available countries and a wide range of weather conditions, with detailed annotations:

  • Countries: All available (India, Japan, USA)
  • Weather Conditions: Snow, rain, sunny, night, and more
  • Damage Types: All annotated types
  • Annotations: Each image is labeled with bounding boxes and the damage type

What You'll Learn & Do

  • Advance the state of the art in robust computer vision for real-world deployment
  • Experiment with GANs, domain adaptation, and advanced augmentation
  • Develop and evaluate models on diverse, challenging data
  • Contribute to research with societal and scientific impact
