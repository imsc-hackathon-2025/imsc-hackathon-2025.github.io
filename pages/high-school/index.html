High School Track

Building the foundation for future innovators

Designed specifically for high school students (grades 9-12), this track focuses on fundamental programming concepts, creative problem-solving, and introducing students to the exciting world of technology and innovation.

Dataset Timeline Judgement Criteria LeaderBoard

Track Overview

The High School track is designed to be an introduction to competitive programming and technology innovation. We believe in nurturing young talent and providing a supportive environment for high school students to explore their passion for technology.

What Makes This Track Special?

  • Beginner-Friendly: No prior hackathon experience required
  • Mentorship Focus: Dedicated mentors for each team
  • Learning-Oriented: Emphasis on learning over competition
  • Creative Freedom: Build anything that excites you
