Challenge Overview

Welcome to the Smart City Traffic Prediction Challenge! Your task is to develop a machine learning model that can predict traffic congestion levels in urban areas using historical traffic data, weather conditions, and city events.

Goals

  • Predict traffic congestion levels for the next 2 hours
  • Achieve minimum 75% accuracy on test data
  • Create visualizations showing traffic patterns
  • Build a simple web interface for predictions

Dataset Structure

traffic_data.csv

Size: 1.8 GB | Records: 850,000

Historical traffic flow data from 500 intersections across the city

timestamp intersection_id vehicle_count avg_speed congestion_level

weather_data.csv

Size: 0.5 GB | Records: 175,000

Weather conditions affecting traffic patterns

timestamp temperature humidity precipitation visibility

events_data.json

Size: 0.2 GB | Records: 5,000

City events that impact traffic flow

event_id event_type start_time location impact_radius

Key Features

Temporal Patterns

Data spans 2 years with hourly granularity, capturing daily, weekly, and seasonal patterns

Spatial Coverage

500 intersections across different city zones: downtown, residential, commercial, and industrial

Multi-modal Data

Combines traffic sensors, weather stations, and event calendars for comprehensive analysis

Balanced Dataset

Even distribution across congestion levels (1-5) and time periods for fair model training

Evaluation Setup

Data Split

Training 70%
Validation 15%
Test 15%

Evaluation Metrics

  • Primary: Classification Accuracy
  • Secondary: F1-Score (weighted)
  • Bonus: Mean Absolute Error for regression
