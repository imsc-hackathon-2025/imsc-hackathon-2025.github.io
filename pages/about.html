About IMSC Hackathon 2025

Innovation, Collaboration, and Impact in Data Science

Our Mission

The IMSC Hackathon 2025 is designed to foster innovation in data science and machine learning across different educational levels. We believe that groundbreaking solutions can come from minds at any stage of their academic journey.

What Makes Us Different

  • Multi-Level Competition: Separate tracks for High School, Undergraduate, and Graduate+ participants
  • Real-World Datasets: Work with actual industry and research datasets
  • Mentorship Program: Access to industry experts and academic researchers
  • Skill Development: Workshops and tutorials throughout the event

Competition Structure

Our hackathon is structured into three distinct tracks, each tailored to the skill level and experience of participants:

High School Track

Introduction to data science with guided challenges and extensive mentorship. Perfect for students new to programming and data analysis.

Undergraduate Track

Intermediate-level challenges focusing on machine learning fundamentals and practical applications. Designed for students with basic programming experience.

Graduate+ Track

Advanced challenges involving cutting-edge research problems, complex datasets, and novel algorithmic approaches. For experienced researchers and professionals.

Event Timeline

Phase 1: Registration & Team Formation

May 30 - June 5, 2025

Register for your track, form teams (optional), and access preliminary materials.

Phase 2: Dataset Release & Exploration

June 6 - June 13, 2025

Datasets are released, teams can explore the data and plan their approach.

Phase 3: Main Competition

June 14 - June 16, 2025

48-hour intensive coding and analysis period with live support and mentorship.

Phase 4: Judging & Awards

June 16 - June 20, 2025

Expert panel reviews submissions, winners announced, and awards distributed.

Prizes & Recognition

Track Winners

  • 1st Place (Each Track): $3,000 + Internship Opportunities
  • 2nd Place (Each Track): $2,000 + Mentorship Program
  • 3rd Place (Each Track): $1,000 + Certificate of Excellence

Special Awards

  • Best Innovation Award: $2,500
  • Best Social Impact: $2,500
  • Best Technical Implementation: $2,500
