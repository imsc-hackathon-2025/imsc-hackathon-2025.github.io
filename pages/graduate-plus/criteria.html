Evaluation Criteria

Graduate+ track submissions will be evaluated based on advanced technical excellence, research contribution, and real-world impact potential.

🔬

Research Innovation

Weight: 25%
  • Novel approach or methodology
  • Theoretical contribution to the field
  • Integration of cutting-edge techniques
  • Comparison with state-of-the-art methods
  • Publication-quality insights
⚡

Technical Excellence

Weight: 25%
  • Advanced model architecture design
  • Sophisticated feature engineering
  • Optimization and efficiency
  • Scalability considerations
  • Code quality and documentation
🎯

Performance Metrics

Weight: 20%
  • Accuracy and precision
  • Model robustness and generalization
  • Statistical significance of results
  • Cross-validation performance
  • Error analysis and interpretation
🌍

Real-World Impact

Weight: 15%
  • Practical applicability
  • Societal benefit potential
  • Industry relevance
  • Deployment feasibility
  • Ethical considerations
📊

Presentation & Documentation

Weight: 15%
  • Clear methodology explanation
  • Comprehensive technical report
  • Professional presentation quality
  • Reproducibility documentation
  • Research paper format

Submission Requirements

Technical Report

Comprehensive 8-12 page research paper including methodology, experiments, results, and discussion. Must follow academic conference format.

Source Code

Complete, well-documented codebase with clear instructions for reproduction. Include requirements.txt and setup scripts.

Model Files

Trained model weights, configuration files, and any preprocessing artifacts required for inference.

Presentation

15-minute presentation slides covering key insights, methodology, and results. Be prepared for 10 minutes of technical Q&A.

Demo (Optional)

Live demonstration of your solution, web interface, or interactive visualization showcasing real-time inference.

Reproducibility Package

Docker container or virtual environment setup ensuring judges can reproduce your results independently.

Detailed Scoring Rubric

Criteria Excellent (4) Good (3) Satisfactory (2) Needs Improvement (1)
Research Innovation Groundbreaking approach with significant theoretical contribution Novel methodology with clear improvements over existing work Some innovative elements with modest improvements Incremental changes to existing approaches
Technical Excellence State-of-the-art implementation with exceptional engineering Advanced techniques with solid implementation Good technical approach with minor issues Basic implementation with technical limitations
Performance Superior results with statistical significance Strong performance with good validation Acceptable results with adequate testing Limited performance or validation
Impact Potential High societal impact with clear deployment path Good practical relevance with feasible application Some real-world applicability Limited practical relevance
Presentation Publication-ready documentation and exceptional presentation Clear documentation with professional presentation Adequate documentation and presentation Unclear or incomplete documentation

Expert Judging Panel

Graduate+ submissions will be evaluated by a panel of:

  • Senior faculty members from USC and partner institutions
  • Industry research scientists from leading tech companies
  • Principal investigators from national research labs
  • Editorial board members from top-tier conferences

Tips for Success

Research Depth

Demonstrate deep understanding of the problem domain and position your work within the broader research landscape.

Experimental Rigor

Conduct thorough experiments with proper baselines, ablation studies, and statistical analysis.

Technical Innovation

Focus on novel algorithmic contributions rather than just parameter tuning or standard approaches.

Reproducibility

Ensure your work is fully reproducible with clear documentation and version-controlled code.
