Track Overview

Research Challenge

The Graduate+ track is designed for advanced researchers tackling state-of-the-art problems in machine learning and scientific computing. Participants are expected to deliver research-quality contributions.

  • Novel algorithm development
  • Large-scale distributed computing
  • Multi-objective optimization
  • Publication-ready results
  • Theoretical analysis and proofs

Eligibility Requirements

  • Graduate student, PhD candidate, or industry professional
  • Proof of current status (transcript, employment letter, etc.)
  • Advanced programming skills and ML expertise
  • Research experience or published work preferred
  • Individual or team of up to 3 members
  • Commitment to 72-hour intensive development
  • Present findings to expert panel
