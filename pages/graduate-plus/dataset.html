Research Challenge Overview

The Quantum-Classical Hybrid Computing challenge represents the frontier of computational science. Participants will develop novel algorithms that leverage both quantum and classical computing paradigms to solve previously intractable problems in materials science, optimization, and machine learning.

Research Objectives

  • Develop hybrid quantum-classical algorithms for materials discovery
  • Optimize quantum circuit depth while maintaining computational advantage
  • Create novel variational quantum algorithms with provable guarantees
  • Demonstrate quantum speedup for real-world scientific problems
  • Publish-quality theoretical analysis and experimental validation
