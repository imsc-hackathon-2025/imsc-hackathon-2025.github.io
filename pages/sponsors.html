USC Integrated Media Systems Center

Primary Sponsor

Research Center

University of Southern California

The Integrated Media Systems Center (IMSC) blends the unique resources available across the University of Southern California and Los Angeles to create a world-class, multidisciplinary hub for exploration and discovery. IMSC focuses on data-driven solutions for real-world applications by applying multidisciplinary research in data science, spanning transportation, health, media & entertainment, and smart cities.

Soongsil University

International Partner

Educational Institution

Seoul, Korea

Soongsil University is a prestigious private university established in 1897 in Seoul, Korea. With over 120 years of history, Soongsil has played a leading role in Korean education while serving as a pioneer in specialization and diversification. The university operates under the founding philosophy of 'Truth and Service,' focusing on cultivating global leaders and competent professionals with 4 colleges, 55 departments, and over 17,000 students.
