Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Who can participate?

The hackathon is open to all university students, recent graduates, and young professionals. No prior hackathon experience required!

Do I need a team?

You can participate solo or in teams of 2-4 people. We'll also have team formation sessions during the opening ceremony.

What should I bring?

Bring your laptop, chargers, and any hardware you want to use. We'll provide food, drinks, and workspace.

Is there a registration fee?

No! The event is completely free. We'll provide meals, snacks, swag, and workspace for all participants.

What can I build?

Anything! Web apps, mobile apps, AI/ML models, hardware projects, games, or innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Will there be mentors?

Yes! We have experienced mentors from top tech companies and startups who will help guide your projects.
