2025 IMSC Hackathon

Innovation • Collaboration • Impact

Join us for 2025 IMSC Hackathon, where innovation meets real-world impact! This year’s challenge centers on building intelligent solutions for road damage detection—empowering participants to harness AI and data science to make transportation safer and smarter. Collaborate with bright minds, tackle pressing infrastructure problems, and develop technologies that can transform how we monitor and maintain roads worldwide. Whether you’re a beginner or an expert, this is your chance to push boundaries, learn cutting-edge skills, and create solutions that matter.
